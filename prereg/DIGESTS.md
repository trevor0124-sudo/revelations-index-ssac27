## Note on NFL 2025

Locked 31 August 2026, by which date the 2025 rookie season had concluded — 204 days earlier. That cohort's first professional year was public at scoring time. It is a partial forward test whose value rests on seasons two and three, and it is reported as such rather than as a clean prospective prediction.

The three 2026 cohorts were locked before their players had appeared professionally.

## What a digest establishes

Temporal precedence, not informational independence. It proves scores were fixed before outcomes were consulted. It does not prove they were generated in ignorance of outcomes, and no available procedure could. See PROTOCOL.md.
